Privacy Policy

MJS Design Lab — Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications published by MJS Design Lab ("we", "our") on Google Play, including Scam Alert, Pop It Fidget, Load-Shedding Alert, Merit Calculator, and related apps.

1. Data We Collect

Our apps are designed to work without collecting personal data. Specifically:

  • No account required — you can use our apps without signing up.
  • No personal information — we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or location.
  • On-device processing — features like SMS scam checking run entirely on your device. Your messages never leave your phone.
  • Local storage only — preferences and counters (e.g. trial uses, high scores) are stored only on your device.

2. Advertising

Our free apps may display ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your device's Google Settings.

3. Third-Party Services

Our apps may load content from our website (muntahashaikh7.github.io) to deliver instant updates. Standard web request data (IP address, user agent) may be logged by the hosting provider per their policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no personal data to secure or breach. On-device data is protected by your device's own security.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of our apps after changes constitutes acceptance.
